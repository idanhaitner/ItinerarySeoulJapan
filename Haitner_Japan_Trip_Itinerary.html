Before you go

  • Bring passports for tax-free shopping (usually ¥5,000+ at participating stores).
  • Comfortable walking shoes are essential almost every day.
  • Use luggage forwarding (takkyubin) between cities — especially Tokyo→Kyoto and Kyoto→Osaka.
  • Book timed tickets early: DisneySea, teamLab Planets, Universal Studios, and sumo if available.
  • September can be warm and rainy; pack a light rain jacket and portable charger.

Touchdown in Tokyo

Land at Narita, private transfer to Shinjuku, and ease into Japan with a gentle first evening.

Itinerary

01

Narita Airport Arrival

Private transfer · ~1h 30m

Shinjuku Evening Stroll

Food

Godzilla · neon · first bites

Wander Shinjuku’s shopping streets, spot the Godzilla statue, and feel the city’s energy without a packed agenda. Perfect first taste of Tokyo’s scale and nightlife glow.

Getting around

  • 1Airport → hotel: private driver pickup (~1h 30m).
  • 2Stay near the hotel this evening — no long transit needed.

Recommendations

  • Treat today as jet-lag recovery — early dinner, early sleep.
  • Buy a Suica/Pasmo (or use iPhone Suica) for trains tomorrow.

Day 02 Wednesday · September 2, 2026
Tokyo

Shibuya, Meiji Shrine & Harajuku

Tokyo icons in one walkable arc: the scramble crossing, a forest shrine, Takeshita Street, Cat Street, and Omotesando.

StayKeio Plaza Hotel Tokyo
Today’s food focus
Harajuku crepes on Takeshita; later, café-hop on Omotesando or Cat Street.

Itinerary

01

Shibuya Crossing

Must-see

Scramble · Hachiko · Hikarie view

Cross the famous scramble, meet Hachiko, then ride up Shibuya Hikarie for a free 11th-floor view over the intersection. Miyashita Park is a good rest/shopping pause.

02

Meiji Jingu

Culture

Forest shrine in the city

One of Japan’s great Shinto shrines, wrapped in quiet woodland. Write a wish on an ema plaque. On some days you may see a traditional wedding procession.

03

Takeshita Street & Cat Street

Food

Harajuku youth culture

Takeshita is colorful, crowded, and crepe-famous. Cat Street is calmer and more stylish — today’s Harajuku fashion energy lives here.

04

Omotesando

Tokyo’s tree-lined avenue

Elegant boutiques, architecture, and cafés along a shaded avenue often called Tokyo’s Champs-Élysées. A grown-up contrast to Takeshita Street.

Getting around

  • 1Hotel → Shibuya (~20m): Yamanote Line to Shibuya.
  • 2Shibuya → Meiji Shrine (~15m): Yamanote to Yoyogi, then walk.
  • 3Meiji → Takeshita (~10m walk); Cat Street (~5–10m more).
  • 4Return via Harajuku Station → Shinjuku (~30m).

Recommendations

  • Lots of walking — schedule café breaks.
  • Bring passports for tax-free shopping (usually ¥5,000+).
  • If jet lag hits, you are close enough to return to the hotel midday.

Day 04 Friday · September 4, 2026
Tokyo

Asakusa, Kappabashi & Skytree

Old Tokyo’s temple heart, kitchen-town souvenirs, and Japan’s tallest tower.

StayKeio Plaza Hotel Tokyo
Today’s food focus
Nakamise snacks, melon pan, and Asakusa classics — try Hatoya, Benizuru Pancake, or Fruits Parlor Gotō.

Itinerary

01

Asakusa & Senso-ji

Must-see

Kaminarimon · Nakamise · Kannon temple

Start at the Asakusa Culture Tourist Information Center (Kengo Kuma design, 8th-floor view), walk Nakamise shopping street, then visit vivid Senso-ji — Tokyo’s most beloved temple.

02

Kappabashi Street

Food

Kitchen town

Nearly 1 km of chef-supply shops: knives, cookware, plastic food samples, lanterns. Excellent practical souvenirs for food lovers.

03

Tokyo Skytree & Solamachi

Must-see

634m views

Observation decks at 350m / 450m. Clear days may reveal Mt. Fuji. Shop and eat in Solamachi at the base.

Getting around

  • 1Hotel → Asakusa (~35–45m): subway via Asakusa Line / transfers.
  • 2Senso-ji → Kappabashi: ~10m walk.
  • 3Kappabashi → Skytree (~25m): Tobu Skytree Line.
  • 4Return to Shinjuku by subway/JR (~40–50m).

Recommendations

  • Senso-ji is busiest ~16:00–18:30 — earlier is calmer.
  • Buy a quality kitchen knife at Kappabashi if you want one keepsake.
  • Book Skytree tickets online to skip some queues.

Day 05 Saturday · September 5, 2026
Tokyo

Tsukiji breakfast, palace & Ginza

A food-first morning at Tsukiji Outer Market, then Imperial Palace gardens and polished Ginza shopping.

StayKeio Plaza Hotel Tokyo
Today’s food focus
Tsukiji breakfast is the main event — tamagoyaki, grilled seafood, fresh sashimi bowls.

Itinerary

01

Tsukiji Outer Market

Food

Tokyo’s legendary food stroll

The outer market remains one of Tokyo’s best breakfast experiences. Graze stall to stall — egg omelets, seafood bowls, grilled scallops — before the day crowds thicken.

02

Imperial Palace East Gardens

Culture

Quiet green heart of the capital

Free gardens on the former castle grounds — moats, walls, lawns, and a calm contrast to central Tokyo. Pair with a Marunouchi café stop afterward.

03

Marunouchi & Ginza

Elegant shopping streets

Shin-Marunouchi Building, Nakadori Avenue, then Ginza: Uniqlo flagship, GINZA SIX, Mitsukoshi rooftop terrace, Ginza Loft, and Kabuki-za. Architecture itself is part of the fun.

Getting around

  • 1Hotel → Tsukiji (~35–40m by subway).
  • 2Tsukiji → Imperial Palace East Gardens: walk / short subway.
  • 3Palace area → Ginza: easy walk via Marunouchi.
  • 4Return to Shinjuku by subway/JR.

Recommendations

  • Arrive Tsukiji early (before 9:00) for the best energy.
  • Passport for tax-free Ginza shopping.
  • Keep the afternoon flexible — this is a rich but walkable day.

Day 07 Monday · September 7, 2026
Tokyo

Ueno, Akihabara & Golden Gai

Market grazing in Ameyoko, pop culture in Akihabara, tiny bars in Golden Gai.

StayKeio Plaza Hotel Tokyo
Today’s food focus
Arrive Ameyoko hungry. Pre-Golden Gai dinner idea: Yakitori Izakaya Gekibutori.

Itinerary

01

Ameyoko Market (Ueno)

Food

Street-food market under the tracks

Lively stalls between Okachimachi and Ueno — seafood skewers, fruit, snacks, and market chaos in the best way. Most shops roughly 10:00–20:00.

02

Ueno Park (optional add-on)

Culture

Museums & temple pause

If you want culture between snacks: Ueno Park holds major museums and shrines. Even a short stroll resets the pace before Akihabara.

03

Akihabara Electric Town

Park

Arcades · anime · maid cafés

Electronics, gaming, and otaku culture. Try GiGO / TAITO / Namco arcades. For a playful stop: @home café or Maidreamin — funny even for one coffee.

04

Golden Gai Night Out

Must-see

Shinjuku’s tiny bar maze

Dozens of microscopic bars with unique personalities. Enter one or two, order a drink, and enjoy the intimate Tokyo nightlife classic.

Getting around

  • 1Hotel → Ameyoko (~40m): Yamanote to Okachimachi.
  • 2Ameyoko → Akihabara (~15m): Yamanote.
  • 3Akihabara → hotel (~45m), then walk ~15m to Golden Gai.

Recommendations

  • Some Golden Gai bars are Japanese-only or regulars-only — just try the next door.
  • Expect a one-drink minimum; sometimes a small cover.
  • Arcades are cash-friendly — keep coins handy.

Itinerary

01

Hakone Open-Air Museum

Culture

Sculpture in the mountains

Outdoor sculptures against valley views, plus indoor galleries. Easy to spend calm hours combining art and mountain air.

02

Owakudani

Active volcanic valley

Sulfur vents and hot springs. On clear days, Mt. Fuji appears. Taste the famous black eggs.

Recommendations

  • Start early — Hakone transfers take time.
  • If behind schedule, skip the Open-Air Museum.

Torii tunnel mountain paths

Food

Culture

Chanoyu with matcha, precise ritual, and English explanation — a peaceful cultural anchor for the day.

Recommendations

  • Arrive Fushimi early — it gets extremely busy.
  • Comfortable shoes for continuous climbing.
  • Sip slowly in Uji; this day is about taste and atmosphere.

Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)

Must-see

Japan’s most famous temple view

Ryoan-ji or Nijo Castle

Culture

Choose your culture chapter

Food

Shopping · cafés · second looks

Return south for shopping around Shijo/Kawaramachi, another Nishiki tasting lap, or simply sit by the Kamogawa with a drink. Luggage tip: forward bags to Osaka tonight/tomorrow.

Getting around

  • 1Hotel → Kinkaku-ji: bus ~40–50m (or taxi).
  • 2Kinkaku-ji → Ryoan-ji: short bus/taxi.
  • 3Return to central Kyoto by bus.

Recommendations

  • Kinkaku-ji is very popular — earlier is better.
  • Forward luggage to Cross Hotel Osaka before Hiroshima.
  • Keep tomorrow’s transfer bags light.

Day 16 Wednesday · September 16, 2026
Hiroshima

Miyajima Island day

Floating torii, island snacks, and Mt. Misen views — one of Japan’s most beautiful day trips.

StayDaiwa Roynet Hotel Hiroshima
Today’s food focus
Momiji manju maple cakes, fresh oysters (season), and grilled conger eel along Omotesando.

Itinerary

01

Omotesando Shopping Street

Food

Island food & souvenirs

About 350m of shops leading to the shrine — wooden rice paddles, maple-leaf cakes, and seafood stalls.

02

Itsukushima Shrine

Must-see

Visit at high tide and low tide if you can

The world-famous “floating” torii and shrine on stilts. High tide = classic floating look; low tide = walk near the gate. Check tide times in advance.

03

Mt. Misen

Park

Ropeway + summit hike

Island high point with Seto Inland Sea panoramas. Ropeway up, then extra walking to viewpoints. See Reikado’s eternal flame tradition.

Getting around

  • 1Hotel → Miyajimaguchi (~55m): tram + JR.
  • 2Ferry ~10m; island sights mostly on foot + ropeway.
  • 3Return same way (~50m to hotel after ferry).

Recommendations

  • Leave early to beat island crowds.
  • Check tide tables — it changes the shrine experience.
  • Good shoes for Misen’s extra climb beyond the ropeway.
