# 🇰🇷 SOUTH KOREA (Aug 27 – Sep 1)

### Day 0: Aug 27 | Arrival & Night Walk

* **Afternoon:** Airport transfer, check-in to hotel, rest and refresh.
* **Evening:** Explore **Myeongdong** street food night market and shopping streets.
* **Late Night (Optional):** If energy permits, head over to **Hongdae** for late-night drinks and street performances.

---

### Day 1: Aug 28 | Royal Heritage & Historic Alleys

* **Morning (Pick Option A or B):**
* **Option A (Classic):** Cafe breakfast in Hongdae $\rightarrow$ **Gyeongbokgung Palace** (rent Hanbok outside for free palace entry).
* **Option B (Scenic & Peaceful):** Breakfast in Hongdae $\rightarrow$ **Eunpyeong Hanok Village** (less crowded hanok town with Mt. Bukhansan views) $\rightarrow$ **Gyeongbokgung Palace**.


* **Afternoon:** Stroll through **Bukchon Hanok Village**, then explore the aesthetic cafes and boutique shops of **Ikseon-dong** & **Insadong**.
* **Evening:** **Gwangjang Market** for a traditional street food feast (mung bean pancakes, *mayak kimbap*, knife-cut noodles).

---

### Day 2: Aug 29 | Thrills, Views & Nightlife

* **Morning:** Grab a quick convenience store breakfast $\rightarrow$ **Lotte World Theme Park** (open 10:00–22:00).
* **Afternoon:** Lunch at Lotte World Mall $\rightarrow$ Walk around **Seokchon Lake Park** $\rightarrow$ Head up **Lotte World Tower (SEOUL SKY)** for 360° city views.
* **Evening:** Relax at a traditional **Jjimjilbang** (Korean Korean Spa/Sauna) to unwind after walking.
* **Night:** Return to hotel to freshen up $\rightarrow$ Night out in **Hongdae** (bars, indie music, clubs).

---

### Day 3: Aug 30 | Royalty, K-Beauty & Night Shopping

* **Morning:** Relaxed morning at a specialty aesthetic cafe in Hongdae $\rightarrow$ Quick visit to **Jogyesa Temple** $\rightarrow$ **Changdeokgung Palace & Secret Garden** *(⚠️ Must pre-book Secret Garden tickets online!)*.
* **Afternoon:** Transit to Gangnam for skin/beauty treatment #1 $\rightarrow$ **Bongeunsa Temple** $\rightarrow$ **COEX Mall & Starfield Library**.
* **Evening:** Head to Dongdaemun to visit the **Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP)** and shop at overnight fashion malls (*APM, DDP Fashion Mall, Hyundai City Outlets*).

---

### Day 4: Aug 31 | Hipster Seoul, Views & Itaewon Night

* **Morning/Afternoon:** Explore **Seongsu-dong** (Seoul’s trendiest neighborhood, boutique shopping, Seongsu Yeonbang) $\rightarrow$ Skincare/Beauty treatment #2.
* **Late Afternoon:** Bus or cable car up to **N Seoul Tower (Namsan)** for sunset.
* **Evening/Night:** Walk down to **Itaewon** for dinner, international bars, and vibrant LGBTQ+ nightlife around Itaewon's Homo Hill.

---

### Day 5: Sep 1 | Seoul to Tokyo Transfer

* Pack up, check out, transfer to Incheon (ICN) / Gimpo (GMP) airport, and fly to Tokyo!

---

# 🇯🇵 JAPAN (Sep 1 – Sep 24)

## 📍 TOKYO: PART 1 (Sep 1 – Sep 6)

### Day 6: Sep 1 | Arrival in Tokyo & Shinjuku Lights

* **Afternoon:** Land in Tokyo, pick up Suica/Pasmo transit card / eSIM, check into hotel.
* **Evening:** Explore **Shinjuku** $\rightarrow$ Dinner in **Omoide Yokocho** (Memory Lane) or **Golden Gai** alleyway bars.

### Day 7: Sep 2 | West Tokyo: Culture, Pop & Sunset Views

* **Morning:** **Meiji Jingu Shrine** $\rightarrow$ Walk down **Takeshita Street** (Harajuku) & **Omotesando**.
* **Afternoon:** Explore **Shibuya** (Shibuya Crossing, Shibuya Center-gai, Megastores).
* **Late Afternoon/Evening:** Pre-booked sunset ticket for **Shibuya Sky** deck $\rightarrow$ Dinner & drinks in Shibuya.

### Day 8: Sep 3 | Historic Tokyo & Electric Town

* **Morning:** Early morning visit to **Senso-ji Temple** in Asakusa $\rightarrow$ Walk through Nakamise Shopping Street.
* **Afternoon:** Walk/transit to **Tokyo Skytree** OR **Ueno Park** $\rightarrow$ Head to **Akihabara** for gaming, anime culture, and multi-story electronics shops.
* **Evening:** Izakaya dinner in Akihabara or Kanda.

### Day 9: Sep 4 | Digital Art & Bay Views

* **Morning:** **Tsukiji Outer Market** for fresh seafood/sushi breakfast.
* **Afternoon:** Visit **teamLab Planets** (Toyosu) or **teamLab Borderless** (Azabudai Hills) *(pre-booking required)* $\rightarrow$ Explore **Odaiba** waterfront and Giant Gundam.
* **Evening:** Upscale walk and dinner in **Ginza**.

### Day 10: Sep 5 | Magic & Coastal Culture

* **Option A:** Full day at **Tokyo DisneySea** (book tickets 2 months in advance).
* **Option B:** Visit **Ghibli Museum** (Kichijoji) $\rightarrow$ Inokashira Park $\rightarrow$ Shopping in **Shimokitazawa** (vintage/thrift shops).

---

## ♨️ HAKONE (Sep 6 – Sep 7)

### Day 11: Sep 6 | Romancecar & Volcanic Valleys

* **09:00:** Board the **Odakyu Romancecar** from Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto (~80 mins, ~2,470 JPY).
* **Afternoon:** Take the Hakone Ropeway to **Owakudani** volcanic valley (eat the black eggs for good luck, views of Mt. Fuji).
* **Late Afternoon:** Check in to a traditional **Ryokan** $\rightarrow$ Soak in natural **Onsen** hot springs $\rightarrow$ Multi-course *Kaiseki* dinner.

---

## 🗻 KAWAGUCHIKO / FUJI FIVE LAKES (Sep 7 – Sep 9)

### Day 12: Sep 7 | Torii Gate Sunrise & Fuji Shorelines

* **Early Morning:** Visit **Hakone Shrine** to catch the famous floating *Heiwa no Torii* gate before crowd surges.
* **Morning:** Lake Ashi Pirate Ship Cruise $\rightarrow$ Return to Hakone-Yumoto.
* **Transit to Kawaguchiko (~1.5–2 hours):**
* Bus from Hakone-Yumoto/Sengoku to **Gotemba Station** (35–45 mins).
* Direct Fujikyu Bus from Gotemba to **Fujisan Station / Kawaguchiko** (45–50 mins). *Cost: ~1,600–2,100 JPY*.


* **Afternoon:** Stop by the famous **Lawson Fuji View** spot $\rightarrow$ Hotel Check-in $\rightarrow$ Sunset walk at **Oishi Park** on Lake Kawaguchiko.

### Day 13: Sep 8 | Sunrise Pagoda & Fuji-Q Thrills

* **Sunrise:** Early taxi/drive to **Chureito Pagoda** (Arakurayama Sengen Park) for iconic Mt. Fuji sunrise photos.
* **All Day:** **Fuji-Q Highland** theme park (rollercoasters like Do-Dodonpa, Takabisha, Super Scary Labyrinth of Fear).
* **Evening:** Relax, local Hoto noodle dinner, early sleep.

---

## 🚅 KAWAGUCHIKO TO KYOTO (Sep 9)

### Day 14: Sep 9 | Transfer to Kansai & Historic Gion

> 💡 **Recommended Route (Mishima Shinkansen):** > * Express Bus from Kawaguchiko to **Mishima Station** (~90 mins).
> * Tokaido Shinkansen from Mishima to **Kyoto Station** (~110 mins).
> * *Total:* ~3.5–4 hours | ~12,500–13,500 JPY. (Far more comfortable than an overnight bus!).
> 
> 

* **Afternoon:** Arrive in Kyoto, drop bags at hotel, walk through tranquil **Kennin-ji Temple**.
* **Evening:** Stroll through **Gion** (Geisha district) $\rightarrow$ Riverside dinner along **Pontocho Alley** by the Kamogawa River.

---

## ⛩️ KYOTO (Sep 10 – Sep 14)

### Day 15: Sep 10 | Torii Gates & Ancient Slopes

* **Early Sunrise (06:30–07:00):** **Fushimi Inari Taisha** (thousands of red Torii gates; hike higher up to beat crowds).
* **Late Morning:** Cafe break $\rightarrow$ **Kiyomizu-dera Temple**.
* **Afternoon:** Walk down historical preserved streets **Sannenzaka & Ninenzaka** $\rightarrow$ **Nishiki Market** for street food snacks $\rightarrow$ Shopping around Shijo-Kawaramachi.

### Day 16: Sep 11 | Arashiyama Bamboo & Monkey Park

* **Morning:** Take train to **Otagi Nenbutsu-ji** (1,200 unique stone rakan statues) & **Adashino Nenbutsu-ji** $\rightarrow$ Walk through historic **Saga Toriimoto Street**.
* **Midday:** **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** $\rightarrow$ Tenryu-ji Temple $\rightarrow$ **Kimono Forest** at Arashiyama Station.
* **Afternoon:** Lunch near **Togetsukyo Bridge** $\rightarrow$ Hike up **Iwatayama Monkey Park** for panoramic city views.
* **Evening:** Return to central Kyoto for dinner.

### Day 17: Sep 12 | Mountain Hike & River Dining Day Trip (Kurama & Kibune)

* **Morning:** Train from Demachiyanagi Station to **Kurama Station** via Eizan Railway (~30 mins, ~430 JPY) $\rightarrow$ Visit **Kurama-dera Temple** on the mountain.
* **Midday:** Easy 45-minute mountain trail hike through the forest down to **Kibune Village**.
* **Afternoon:** Visit **Kifune Shrine** (red lantern stairs) $\rightarrow$ Experience **Kawadoko** lunch (dining on wooden decks built directly over the rushing river stream).
* **Late Afternoon:** Relax at local **Kurama Onsen** (if open) $\rightarrow$ Return train back to Kyoto.

### Day 18: Sep 13 | Zen Gardens & Golden Pavilion

* **Morning:** **Ginkaku-ji** (Silver Pavilion) $\rightarrow$ Peaceful walk along **The Philosopher’s Path**.
* **Midday:** **Nanzen-ji Temple** & aqueduct $\rightarrow$ Lunch nearby.
* **Late Afternoon:** Short taxi ride to **Kinkaku-ji** (Golden Pavilion) to catch golden hour sunlight on the temple.
* **Evening:** Farewell Kyoto dinner.

---

## 🏯 OSAKA & DAY TRIPS (Sep 14 – Sep 19)

### Day 19: Sep 14 | Transfer to Osaka & Street Food Heaven

* **Morning:** 30-minute train ride from Kyoto to Osaka $\rightarrow$ Check into hotel.
* **Afternoon:** Explore **Osaka Castle Park** $\rightarrow$ Walk through **Shinsekai** & Tsutenkaku Tower.
* **Evening:** **Dotonbori** street food crawl (Takoyaki, Okonomiyaki) $\rightarrow$ Photos at the Glico Running Man sign $\rightarrow$ **Hozenji Yokocho** alley.

### Day 20: Sep 15 | Universal Studios Japan (USJ)

* **All Day:** **Universal Studios Japan** (Super Nintendo World & Wizarding World of Harry Potter). *(⚠️ Pre-book Express Passes 2 months prior!)*.

### Day 21: Sep 16 | Nara Deer Park Day Trip

* **Morning:** 45-minute train to **Nara**.
* **Day Highlights:** Walk through **Nara Park** (bowing deer) $\rightarrow$ **Todai-ji Temple** (Great Giant Buddha) $\rightarrow$ **Kasuga Taisha Shrine**.
* **Evening:** Return to Osaka for shopping in **Shinsaibashi** & dinner in Amerikamura.

### Day 22: Sep 17 | Hiroshima & Miyajima Day Trip

* **Morning:** Early Shinkansen to Hiroshima (~80 mins).
* **Highlights:** **Miyajima Island** (Itsukushima Shrine floating Torii Gate) $\rightarrow$ **Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park & Museum**.
* **Evening:** Try authentic Hiroshima-style Okonomiyaki before returning to Osaka.

### Day 23: Sep 18 | Umeda & Modern Osaka

* **Morning:** Shopping in **Umeda** (Hep Five, Grand Front Osaka).
* **Late Afternoon:** Sunset view from **Umeda Sky Building** / Kuchu Teien Observatory.
* **Evening:** Drinks in **Namba** or Nakazakicho hipster quarter.

---

## 🗼 TOKYO: PART 2 & DEPARTURE (Sep 19 – Sep 24)

### Day 24: Sep 19 | Return to Tokyo & Nightlife

* **Morning:** Shinkansen back to Tokyo (~2.5 hours).
* **Afternoon:** Check-in $\rightarrow$ Explore **Roppongi** or **Azabudai Hills** complex.
* **Evening:** Nighttime photos at **Tokyo Tower** $\rightarrow$ Kabukicho arcade & bar hopping.

### Day 25: Sep 20 | Subcultures & Vintage Shopping

* **Morning:** Relaxing walk in **Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden**.
* **Afternoon:** Thrift shopping in **Shimokitazawa** or pop-culture hunting at **Nakano Broadway**.
* **Evening:** Specialty Wagyu BBQ dinner.

### Day 26: Sep 21 | Kamakura Coastal Day Trip (Optional)

* **Day Trip:** 1-hour train to coastal **Kamakura** (Kotoku-in Great Buddha, Enoden coastal train line, beach view cafes).
* **Evening:** Return to Tokyo for dinner.

### Day 27: Sep 22 | Last-Minute Shopping & Fine Dining

* **All Day:** Souvenir shopping in **Ginza**, **Akihabara**, or **Shibuya**.
* **Night:** Farewell celebratory dinner in Tokyo.

### Day 28: Sep 23/24 | Pack & Departure

* Pack suitcases, catch Narita Express (N'EX) or Tokyo Monorail to Narita (NRT) / Haneda (HND) airport, and fly home!
